[b]Automata: Plug-in Chips[/b] adds permanent character powerups earned every time you take down a boss.

Instead of bosses just dropping standard loot, you get to pick from powerful powerups that stick with your character for the whole run. You can stack them up to create crazy builds, turn yourself into an aggressive lifesteal tank, or run around healing your friends with the new Medi Gun.

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
Kill a boss, pick 1 of 3 powerups, and it is permanently bound to your character. No inventory clutter, no accessory slots wasted. The mod also tracks what you already have so you never get offered duplicates.

[h2]The Medi Gun (TF2 Style Healing!)[/h2]
[img]https://i.imgur.com/c5RV7ed.gif[/img]

Wanted to play actual medic in Terraria so I made this!
Craft MK I early game at an anvil using a Life Crystal, Gold or Platinum bars, and a Fallen Star. In Hardmode, upgrade to MK II with Crystal Shards and Souls of Light, and forge the ultimate MK III Post-Plantera with Spectre Bars and Life Fruits for rapid 10 HP/s healing and maximum range!

Hold left click near a teammate to lock on with a bending energy tether that heals them constantly while you dodge boss attacks together. Super fun for co-op runs, and modular upgrades are coming soon so you can slot accessories into it for extra buffs!

[h2]Example Plug-in Chips[/h2]
[b]Godslayer Blade[/b] (Legendary Melee): Melee crits summon a colossal falling blade that crushes targets for 75% weapon damage and shreds them with Ichor.
[b]Ricochet Engine[/b] (Legendary Ranged): Ranged hits spawn ricochet rounds that bounce between enemies and trigger on-hit effects.
[b]Queen's Swarm[/b] (Legendary Summon): Minion strikes build infestation stacks, bursting into a vicious swarm of void bugs.
[b]Time Warp[/b] (Epic Magic): Magic crits distort temporal flow, slowing all nearby enemies by 40% for 3 seconds.
[b]Chain Lightning[/b] (Rare Melee): Crits arc bolts of high-voltage lightning to up to 2 nearby enemies for 50% damage.
[b]Second Wind[/b] (Common Universal): Dropping below 25% HP instantly triggers an emergency heal for 40 health.
[b]Ironclad Aura[/b] (Support): Radiates +8 defense to all nearby allies during multiplayer boss battles.
[b]Master Angler[/b] (Utility): Grants +10 Fishing Power and gives opened crates a 20% chance for bonus gold loot.
