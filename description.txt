[b]Automata: Plug-in Chips[/b] adds permanent character powerups earned every time you take down a boss.

Instead of bosses just dropping standard loot, you get to pick from powerful powerups that stick with your character for the whole run. You can stack them up to create crazy builds, turn yourself into an aggressive lifesteal tank, or run around healing your friends with the new Medi Gun.

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
Kill a boss, pick 1 of 3 powerups, and it is permanently bound to your character. No inventory clutter, no accessory slots wasted. The mod also tracks what you already have so you never get offered duplicates.

[h2]The Medi Gun (TF2 Style Healing!)[/h2]
Wanted to play actual medic in Terraria so I made this!
Craft it early game at an anvil using a Life Crystal, Gold or Platinum bars, and a Fallen Star.

Hold left click near a teammate to lock on with a bending energy tether that heals them constantly while you dodge boss attacks together. Super fun for co-op runs, and modular upgrades are coming soon so you can slot accessories into it for extra buffs!

[h2]Example Powerups[/h2]
[b]Second Wind[/b]: Heals a chunk of HP when you drop dangerously low
[b]Bloodletter[/b]: Melee crits make enemies bleed out over time
[b]Vampiric Edge[/b]: Melee kills restore your health
[b]Sunder[/b]: Melee crits shred enemy defense with Ichor
[b]Ambush[/b]: Stand still to charge up a huge burst hit
[b]Riposte[/b]: Take a hit, hit right back for heavy bonus damage
[b]Momentum Swing[/b]: Keep attacking the same target to ramp up your damage
